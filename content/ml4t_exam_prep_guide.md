# ML4T Exam-1 Prep Guide
## Exam Format and Scoring
Each exam contains 40 multiple-select questions.
Each exam covers 20 domains: 10 Machine Learning domains and 10 Quantitative Finance domains.
Two questions are drawn at random for each student from each domain.
Each question contains five statements or answer choices, each of which must be evaluated independently as True or False.
At least one choice in every question must be True, and at least one choice must be False.
Each question is worth 5 points and consists of 5 sub-questions.
You receive one raw point for each correct True or each correct False choice. Answers marked incorrectly as True, marked incorrectly as False, or not moved into a True or False category will receive zero points.
The exam's total raw score is 200 points and will be reported as a percentage with a maximum of 100%.
Warning: Questions with all 5 sub-questions selected or all 5 sub-questions unselected may receive a 0 for that question set.
