[QF2-D4G1Q1] 
A researcher assigns daily forecasts to 200 stocks and later compares those forecasts with the stocks' realized 
benchmark-relative returns over the stated horizon. Across many dates, the correlation is +0.06. The investment

B. An IC of +0.06 means that every positively forecasted stock earned a positive active return. 
C. A small positive IC can be economically useful when it is stable and applied across many sufficiently independent 
opportunities. 
D. The IC alone establishes the strategy's net proﬁtability after trading costs. 
E. Sampling uncertainty should be considered before concluding that the observed IC represents persistent skill. 
 
Answer Key: 
 
A. True – A positive forecast-outcome correlation indicates correct ordering on average, not certainty for every asset.

errors.

D. False – IC measures forecast quality, whereas net results also depend on portfolio construction, risk, turnover, and 
costs. 
E. True – An estimated correlation can differ from the underlying relationship because of ﬁnite-sample noise and 
dependence.
