# books/ — optional textbook PDFs (bring your own)

No PDFs are included with this app. Each book's copyright and license belong to its authors and
publisher. Even the free books are licensed for personal download, not redistribution.

**What works without any PDFs:** everything. Every question still shows its full answers, concept
explanations, and the book, chapter, section and page to read (taken from the distilled notes in
`content/references/`).

**What PDFs add:** on a desktop browser, clicking a citation opens the book at that page in a side
panel. A 📥 next to a citation means that book's PDF isn't in this folder yet.

## Adding a book

1. Get your own copy from the official source below.
2. Save it here with the **exact filename** shown.
3. Restart the app.

| Book | Official source | Save as |
|---|---|---|
| *An Introduction to Statistical Learning with Applications in Python* (ISLP), James, Witten, Hastie, Tibshirani & Taylor | Free PDF from the authors: <https://www.statlearning.com> | `ISLP_website.pdf` |
| *Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction* (Book 1), Murphy | Free PDF from the author: <https://probml.github.io/pml-book/book1.html> | `Probabilistic Machine Learning.pdf` |
| *Foundations of Machine Learning* (2nd ed.), Mohri, Rostamizadeh & Talwalkar | Free PDF from the authors: <https://cs.nyu.edu/~mohri/mlbook/> | `Foundations of Machine Learning.pdf` |
| *Handbook of Artificial Intelligence and Big Data Applications in Investments*, CFA Institute Research Foundation | Free download from the publisher: <https://rpc.cfainstitute.org> (search the title) | `ai-and-big-data-in-investments.pdf` |
| *Deep Learning: Foundations and Concepts*, Bishop & Bishop | Free to read online at <https://www.bishopbook.com>. A PDF requires your own purchased copy. | `Deep Learning_ Foundations and Concepts-Springer (2024).pdf` |
| *What Hedge Funds Really Do*, Romero & Balch | **Not freely available.** Use your own purchased copy. | `What Hedge Funds Really Do - An Introduction to Portfolio Management.pdf` |

The PDF page numbers in the app match the editions above. A different edition or printing may be
off by a few pages.
